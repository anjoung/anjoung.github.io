About

Andrew Joung is an economist at Tools for Humanity. His research uses quasi-experimental methods and large-scale administrative data to examine the intergenerational transmission of economic shocks and regional labor markets. He has also worked on topics related to the future of self-employment. He received his PhD in Economics from the University of Michigan and his BA in Economics from Vassar College. Previously, he worked at The Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, supporting large online behavioral experiments and randomized control trials at Fortune 500 companies.
