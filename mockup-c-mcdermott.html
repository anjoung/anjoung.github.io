About

Welcome! I'm an economist at Tools for Humanity, where I work on questions at the intersection of labor economics, technology, and economic opportunity. My research uses quasi-experimental methods and large-scale administrative data to study the intergenerational transmission of economic shocks and regional labor markets.

I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Michigan and my BA from Vassar College. Previously, I worked at The Wharton School supporting behavioral experiments and RCTs at Fortune 500 companies. I'm based in San Francisco — always happy to connect.
