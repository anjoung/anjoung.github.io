About

Welcome! I am a labor economist with a current focus on the intergenerational effects of household and local labor market shocks. I have also worked on topics related to labor unions, the minimum wage, and the future of self-employment. Currently, I am pursuing my PhD in Economics at the University of Michigan-Ann Arbor (expected 2026).
