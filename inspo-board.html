Design Elements to Watch For
As you browse these sites, pay attention to these specific elements that will matter most for your redesign:

Typography pairing — Most sites you liked use a serif for headings + sans-serif for body, or vice versa. Note which combos feel right.

Accent color — Even "minimal" sites usually have one accent color for links and highlights. Screenshot any color choices that appeal to you.

Photo treatment — Circular crop? Rectangular? Full-bleed? Subtle border or shadow? Small but important.

Research presentation — How do papers get listed? Abstracts inline or expandable? Grouped by topic or chronological? Links to PDFs, slides, data?

Footer & nav — Minimal top nav with anchor links? Sidebar? How much navigation is enough as your site grows?
