About

Welcome! I lead economic research at Tools for Humanity. My work uses quasi-experimental methods and large-scale administrative data to study the intergenerational transmission of economic shocks and regional labor markets.

I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Michigan and my BA from Vassar College. Previously, I worked at The Wharton School supporting behavioral experiments and RCTs at Fortune 500 companies.

I am moving to San Francisco — always happy to grab coffee and chat about economics, policy, or what you're working on.
