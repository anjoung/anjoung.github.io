PhD Candidate in Economics (Expected July 2026)

University of Michigan - Ann Arbor

Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Michigan. My research interests lie in labor economics and public economics. I use quasi-experimental methods and large-scale administrative data to study the intergenerational transmission of economic shocks and the evolution of regional labor markets. I have also worked on topics related to labor unions, the minimum wage, and the future of self-employment.
